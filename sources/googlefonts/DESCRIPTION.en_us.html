Namche Shadow is a display-oriented sans-serif with rounded inner corners and crisp outer forms. It provides upright and italic styles across a continuous weight range from Thin to Black.

Michael Marte designed the treatment for Ruhm etc., based on Geist Sans by Vercel, Basement Studio, Andrés Briganti, Mateo Zaragoza and its contributors.
