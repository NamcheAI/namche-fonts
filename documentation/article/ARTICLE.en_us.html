Namche Shadow Sans adapts the Geist family for Namche. Its primary sans family uses a custom rounded inner-corner treatment designed by Michael Marte for Ruhm etc.. The derivative is owned by BTLG Holding GmbH. Mono remains an outline-identical renamed Geist Mono derivative. Namche Shape is a new design in three static styles (Metaball, Origin, and Skeleton), generated from the pixel grid of Geist Pixel and designed by Michael Marte for Ruhm etc.

The project is based on Vercel's Geist repository and keeps its build layout, original authorship, and SIL Open Font License. Complete authorship and contribution records are included with the source.
