Namche Shadow Sans is a display-oriented sans-serif based on Vercel's Geist Sans. Michael Marte designed its rounded inner-corner treatment for Ruhm etc.. The companion Namche Shadow Mono remains outline-identical to Geist Mono. Namche Shape is a new design in three static styles (Metaball, Origin, and Skeleton), generated from the pixel grid of Vercel's Geist Pixel.

The Namche Shadow derivative is owned by BTLG Holding GmbH and was developed in collaboration with Ruhm etc.

Geist was created by Vercel in collaboration with Basement Studio, Andrés Briganti, Mateo Zaragoza, and its other contributors. Namche Shadow Sans retains their copyright notices and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
